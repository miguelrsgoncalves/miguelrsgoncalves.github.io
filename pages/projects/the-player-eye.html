The Player's Eye

This thesis focused on the relationship between the design of a protagonist in a video-game's story and how the player interprets the game through its eyes. It involved the creation of a prototype game Limbo's Gaol, which served as a testing playground to gather data for the thesis.

I worked on the thesis during my stay in Sweden, where I took part in the Game Design course at Chalmers University of Technology and my supervisor was Michael Heron.

The thesis was an overall success, arriving at good conclusions and having a project I am proud of.
