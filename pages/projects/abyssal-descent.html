Abyssal Descent: SCP-455

Abyssal Descent: SCP-455 was a university project made by a big team composed of 3 different courses, Software Engineering (code), Visual Arts (visuals) and Multimedia (sound design), where we create a game from scratch. Abyssal Descent: SCP-455 is a 3D horror VR experience inside the world of SCP-455 where the player will be put in the shoes of a SCP foundation agent, sent to investigate a mysterious ship abandoned at sea. As you explore the ship, you will uncover the dark secrets that lie within, and face the horrors as you descend into the abyss.

My team was tasked with the game's sound design. A new challenge to tackle, as none of us had done anything horror related prior. But a challenge we happily faced face on! We had to first wait the first concept and demo shots from our programmers, as well as the first concept art from the artists so we could work on an atmosphere that would go hand in hand with the rest that the player would be experiencing. What we got was this solitary cargo ship in the middle of the ocean with a very rustic and decayed appearance with phantasmagoric vibes all around.

With the first drafts of the whole vibe, we got into work to make this project sound like something!

The Design

The Ambience

The world our game would be inside of would be a very lonely, dark and mysterious one. So the sounds and music would need to fit right in that vibe.

For the music, we decided we would have some synth-based generative music soundtrack, where one of our team members worked on all of the sound types that would be playing together, how they would evolve over time and how they would behave with increased intensity. We then passed the sounds onto FMOD, where we made the sounds play in a random and free manner. This ended up creating a very cool and strange feeling while the player explored through the corridors of the ship.
