Abyssal Descent: SCP-455

Abyssal Descent: SCP-455 was a university project made by a big team composed of 3 different courses, Software Engineering (code), Visual Arts (visuals) and Multimedia (sound design), where we create a game from scratch. Abyssal Descent: SCP-455 is a horror VR experience inside the world of SCP-455 where the player will be put in the shoes of a SCP foundation agent, sent to investigate a mysterious ship abandoned at sea. As you explore the ship, you will uncover the dark secrets that lie within, and face the horrors as you descend into the abyss.
