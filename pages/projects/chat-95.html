Chat95

Chat95 was made as part of a game jam with the theme "Parody", which gave me and my team the idea to make a parody of ChatGPT set in the 90s. But instead of you using ChatGPT, you are ChatGPT instead.

In this universe, which is pretty much like any other universe, your users come in various levels of weird and with unusual requests. You must do your best, and your best needs to be the best.

Like a lot of other jams, since I'm, unfortunately, the sole member with knowledge in Sound Design, I once again got assigned to this part of the game. We were not really aiming for perfection, far from it, but I was what we had.

Sound Design

For a parody of a chill work session set in the 90s, it had to have a cozy, homy feeling. So all sounds had to fit in together, easily flowing with each other.
