You play as Chat95, the new and classy LLM, where you will need to closely read and observe all of the confusing user's requests and do you best to fullfil them.

Chat95

Chat95 was made as part of a game jam with the theme "Parody", which gave my team the idea to make a parody of LLMs (Loyal Literate Monkey) set in the 90s. But instead of you using an LLM, you are one instead.

In this universe, which is pretty much like any other universe, your users come in various levels of weird and with unusual requests. You must do your best, and your best needs to be the best.

Like in a lot of other jams, since I'm the sole member with knowledge in Sound Design, I once again got assigned to this part of the game. We were not really aiming for perfection, far from it, but I was what we had.

Sound Design

For a parody of a chill work session set in the 90s, it had to have a cozy, homy feeling. So all sounds had to fit in together, easily flowing with each other.

For an old PC, it had to have aggressive and effortful fans whenever it was loading something. So I found the worst possible sounding PC fans sample and mixed them into an audible asset. This would play at the boot time and whenever the PC loads the next user, giving the player the 90s grandma computer experience.

The keyboard had to feel weighty but not annoying, so I found an audio of someone spit-firing an old keyboard, spliced individual key presses and mixed the audio for a more suppressed effect.

The same philosophy was applied to other SFX, like the file pickup and drop, where I would use office sounds, such as picking up a paper, and mix them into a OS sound cue.

Overall, I think the Sound Design came out pretty nice and put together. It is hear catching enough, while having a somewhat Lo-Fi vibe to it and living up to the 90s aesthetic.
